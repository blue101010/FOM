# CTFTTOU-268 — ML Privacy Meter

> **Tool ID:** `CTFTTOU-268`  
> **Related techniques/counters:** CTFTTE-AIM-005, CTFTCTE-AIM-005

## Description

Audits the membership-inference privacy risk of a trained model by probing its outputs, quantifying how much memorized training data can be recovered through queries.

# CTFTTOU-319 — Google Earth Pro

> **Tool ID:** `CTFTTOU-319`  
> **Related techniques/counters:** CTFTCTE-OSI-004, CTFTTE-OSI-004

## Description

Free desktop globe for imagery geolocation. Historical Imagery dates construction and
landscape changes against a photo's timestamp. The sun/time slider reproduces shadow
direction and length for a given date. 3D buildings and terrain let a photo's perspective,
skyline and elevation profile be matched from a ground-level viewpoint.

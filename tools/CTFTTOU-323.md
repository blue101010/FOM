# CTFTTOU-323 — Wikimapia

> **Tool ID:** `CTFTTOU-323`  
> **Related techniques/counters:** CTFTCTE-OSI-004, CTFTTE-OSI-004

## Description

Crowdsourced map of user-outlined places (buildings, bridges, monuments, industrial sites)
over satellite imagery, with names and descriptions. It identifies a distinctive landmark
or lists candidate sites of a given kind in a region.

<https://wikimapia.org/>

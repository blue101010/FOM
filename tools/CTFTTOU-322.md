# CTFTTOU-322 — Yandex Images

> **Tool ID:** `CTFTTOU-322`  
> **Related techniques/counters:** CTFTCTE-OSI-004, CTFTTE-OSI-004

## Description

Reverse image search engine. It often outperforms Google Lens on buildings, landscapes and
Eastern European / Asian imagery. Searching crops (signage, plates, a distinctive facade)
finds the original post, articles or near-duplicate photos that carry the location.
